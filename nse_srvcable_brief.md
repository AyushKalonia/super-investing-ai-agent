**Snapshot**  
Sarvottam Cables Ltd (NSE: SRVCABLE) manufactures power cables, wires and conductors. Q1 FY27 revenue jumped 18‑35 % YoY to roughly ₹1.25‑1.43 cr, with profit after tax of ₹82 cr and EBITDA margin slipping to 11.2 % due to higher copper costs【2†L9-L15】【1†L1-L5】. The order book stands at ₹3.9 tr, up 24 % YoY, and a new Bharuch plant (≈30 % capacity boost) is slated for commercial production in Q3 FY27【2†L17-L20】.  

**Bull case**  
- **Strong demand tailwinds:** Power‑transmission and data‑centre projects are driving order growth; data‑centre cables already 9 % of domestic revenue and expected to double in two years【3†L1-L4】.  
- **Robust order book:** ₹3.9 tr order book provides visibility and supports FY27 revenue guidance of 15‑17 % growth【3†L1-L3】.  
- **Capex funded internally:** Bharuch plant capex largely covered by internal accruals; net‑debt‑to‑equity remains low at 0.35×【3†L13-L15】.  
- **Improving export mix:** Export contribution rose to 21 % of revenue, diversifying earnings【2†L21-L22】.  

**Bear case**  
- **Margin pressure from copper:** Copper price up ~14 % cut EBITDA margin by 170 bps; pass‑through to customers may take two quarters【1†L4-L7】【3†L2-L4】.  
- **Rising receivable days:** DSO increased from 78 to 96 days, reflecting slower payments from large state utilities【3†L9-L12】.  
- **GST demand liability:** A ₹46.3 cr GST demand (including interest/penalty) for FY20‑22 has been disclosed; while the company expects no material impact, the outcome is uncertain【5†L1-L5】.  
- **Promoter pledge perception:** A 2024 news piece flagged a 35 % promoter pledge, which could raise governance concerns, though the latest shareholding filing shows pledges have fallen to 4.1 % of promoter holdings【6†L1-L4】.  

**Open questions**  
1. **Revenue figure discrepancy:** Business Daily reports Q1 revenue of ₹1,428 cr, whereas the company filing lists ₹1,248 cr. Which figure reflects the audited result?  
2. **Copper cost pass‑through timeline:** Management expects to pass on copper costs over the next two quarters, but the speed and effectiveness of price adjustments remain unclear.  
3. **GST demand resolution:** No timeline or probability of success for the appeal against the ₹46.3 cr GST demand has been disclosed.  
4. **Future promoter pledging:** The recent drop in pledged shares is encouraging, but will the promoter maintain low pledging levels amid any financing needs?
